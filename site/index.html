Everything you want to do with your clips

Your recorder saves them. Cliptrove makes them easy to find, watch, fix up and share.

One library, every recorder

Clips from all your recorders, grouped by game with cover art. Hover over any clip to preview it.

Mix the audio

Game, Chat and Mic each get their own volume slider, up to 200%. Cliptrove remembers your levels for every clip.

Trim and share

Cut out the good part and save a copy in full quality, or compressed under 18 MB so it fits on Discord.

Nicknames and stars

Name your best moments, star them and filter for them later. Put clips from any recorder under the right game.

See what you play

Charts of what you played and when, your top games, and a guess at which games you'll play next week.

Stays on your PC

No account, no uploads, no tracking. Your clips are never moved or changed.

A proper player

Every clip opens in a player built for game clips:

  • Frame-by-frame stepping, loops, speeds and keyboard shortcuts
  • A separate volume for each recorded track
  • Trim handles, and "keep this trim" so previews play just the good part

Know your habits

See what you played week by week, your top games, when you usually play and a calendar of clip days.

Up next learns from your own history and tells you how often it's been right.

Up and running in a minute

No installer, no account, no sign-up.

Download

Get Cliptrove-…-windows.zip from the latest release.

Unzip and run

Extract it anywhere and double-click Cliptrove.exe. It opens in your browser.

Tell it what you record with

A short setup on the first start asks which apps you clip with. Cliptrove finds their folders for you where it can.

Windows may say it "protected your PC" because Cliptrove isn't code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway. For thumbnails, separate Chat/Mic audio and saving copies, also install the free ffmpeg (winget install Gyan.FFmpeg). The step-by-step guide has screenshots.

Private by design

Cliptrove is a small app that runs on your own PC and shows itself in your browser at a private address only you can reach.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, for personal and other noncommercial use. The source code is public under the PolyForm Noncommercial license. Selling it or using it commercially needs permission.

Which recorders does it work with?

SteelSeries GG (read straight from its library), NVIDIA ShadowPlay / NVIDIA App, AMD ReLive / Adrenalin, Xbox Game Bar, OBS Studio, Medal, and any folder of videos. It works out the game and date from each recorder's file names and folders.

Will it change or move my clips?

No. It only reads them. New files are only written when you save an edited copy, into an "Edited Clips" folder you choose.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

The download is for Windows. Developers can run it from the source code on macOS and Linux, but that isn't tested yet.

Something isn't working

Ask in Discussions or report a bug. Please leave out personal folder names.
